# BioDesign Reference Direction

## Brand Hack Target

BioDesign should synthesize three reference directions:

- Marek Health for clinical authority, dark editorial tone, and serious specialist positioning.
- Hone Health for warmer photography, conversion clarity, and trust proof density.
- The Well NYC for luxury wellness restraint, negative space, and refined serif/sans pairing.

## What To Borrow

### From Marek Health

- Dark base tone.
- Editorial typography.
- Serious clinical authority.
- Premium specialist energy.

### From Hone Health

- Warm photography.
- Clear conversion paths.
- Trust bar density.
- Patient-friendly explanation flow.

### From The Well NYC

- Restrained palette.
- Premium spacing.
- Serif display plus clean sans body.
- Calm luxury wellness tone.

## What Not To Borrow

- Gym-brand aesthetics.
- Supplement-store energy.
- Overly cold hospital visuals.
- Generic medspa softness.
- Heavy blue/teal hex-pattern medical cliches.

## BioDesign Palette Direction

- Near-black warm base.
- Elevated dark surface.
- Warm off-white text.
- Champagne gold accent.
- Women's sections may shift to warm cream and dusty mauve.

## BioDesign Typography Direction

- Display: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Fraunces, or Libre Baskerville.
- Body: DM Sans, Instrument Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Outfit.
- Mono or label: DM Mono if needed.
