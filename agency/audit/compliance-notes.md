# BioDesign Compliance Notes

Use this file for legal, medical, regulatory, platform, and client-specific content constraints.

This is not legal advice. It is a working checklist for human review.

## Industry Constraints

- Longevity medicine.
- Hormone therapy.
- Peptide therapy.
- Weight loss.
- Sexual wellness.
- LegitScript sensitivity.

## Banned Or Risky Claims

- Guaranteed results.
- Cure.
- Eliminate.
- Reverse disease.
- Reverse aging.
- Anti-aging tied to disease prevention.
- FDA-approved unless verified for the specific compound or treatment.
- Clinically proven unless supported by a specific source.
- Purchase-oriented peptide claims.

## Banned Or Risky Image Concepts

- Shirtless subjects.
- Bodybuilding.
- Gym equipment.
- Supplement bottles.
- Blue LED laboratory cliches.
- Before/after injection visuals.
- Athletic performance framing.

## Required Disclaimers

- Peptide pages require human review.
- BPC-157 and Thymosin Beta-4 require extra scrutiny.
- Use individual-results-vary language where relevant.
- Use physician-supervision and consultation-first language.

## Review Requirements

- Human review before publishing peptide pages.
- Human review before publishing hormone therapy pages.
- Human review before publishing sexual wellness pages.
- Human review before publishing weight loss claims.
