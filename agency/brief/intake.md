# BioDesign Clinic Intake

## Client

- Name: BioDesign Clinic
- Destination URL: biodesignclinic.com
- Industry: Men's and women's longevity medicine, hormone therapy, peptide therapy
- Locations: Tampa, Orlando, Melbourne, Lake Mary

## Business Goal

Build a unified premium clinical website for BioDesign that presents men's and women's longevity medicine under one stronger brand system.

The immediate Day 1 goal is visible progress: a homepage direction and one location page direction that can become a staging demo.

## Positioning

BioDesign should feel like premium clinical authority: evidence-based, physician-led, refined, warm, and trustworthy.

## Anti-Positioning

BioDesign should not feel like:

- A gym brand.
- A supplement store.
- A generic medspa.
- A spa.
- A cold hospital system.

## Primary Audience

- Men and women seeking longevity, hormone, peptide, weight loss, and wellness care.
- Adults roughly 35-65.
- Patients who value physician-led care and a more premium clinical experience.
- Existing BioDesign Men and BioDesign Women audiences that need a unified brand experience.

## Offers

### Men's

- Testosterone Replacement Therapy.
- Peptide Therapy.
- Weight Loss.
- Hormone Optimization.
- Performance Medicine.

### Women's

- Hormone Replacement.
- Weight Loss.
- Peptide Therapy.
- Sexual Wellness.
- Menopause Management.

## Primary CTA

Free Consultation.

Preferred CTA language:

- Schedule a Free Consultation.
- Start With a Free Consult.
- Book Your Consultation.

## Lead Flow

Form -> Podium -> SMS to patient -> scheduler -> EMR.

## Trust Signals To Gather

- Patient count.
- Average rating.
- Physician-led care proof.
- Years in practice.
- Provider credentials.
- Location details.
- LegitScript-related remediation or compliance notes.

## Brand Notes

- Existing logo: refined circle monogram plus clean serif wordmark.
- Current men's site problem: dark hex pattern and teal aesthetic feels too gym/sci-fi and fights the logo.
- Women's/wellness side: warm creams and neutrals are closer to the unified direction.
- Target feeling: premium longevity medicine, clinical but warm, editorial authority.

## Reference Brands

- Marek Health.
- Hone Health.
- The Well NYC.

## Compliance Constraints

- LegitScript suspended.
- No shirtless imagery.
- No bodybuilding aesthetic.
- No gym-equipment visual framing.
- No supplement-store framing.
- No efficacy claims.
- No guaranteed results.
- No purchase-oriented peptide CTAs.
- Peptide pages need FDA/disclaimer review.
