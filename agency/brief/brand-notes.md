# BioDesign Brand Notes

Use this file to capture the client's existing brand and the upgraded direction.

## Existing Assets

- Logo: refined circle monogram plus clean serif wordmark.
- Current colors: men's side appears dark/teal with hex patterns; women's/wellness side uses warmer creams and neutrals.
- Current fonts: to verify from existing sites.
- Current website: legacy men's and women's brand experiences need unification.
- Existing photography: to gather.
- Brand guidelines: not yet confirmed.

## What To Preserve

- The refined logo signal.
- The sense of medical credibility.
- The separate men's and women's audience paths.

## What To Upgrade

- Replace gym/sci-fi dark teal energy with luxury-clinical editorial authority.
- Create a unified visual system across men's and women's pages.
- Improve trust density and conversion clarity.
- Make imagery warmer, more human, and more compliant.

## Desired Perception

Visitors should describe BioDesign as premium, physician-led, evidence-based, modern, warm, trustworthy, and serious about long-term health.

## Typography Direction

- Display: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Fraunces, or Libre Baskerville.
- Sans: DM Sans, Instrument Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans, or Outfit.
- Serif: use for editorial H1/H2, not body-heavy paragraphs.
- Notes: high-contrast serif plus clean geometric sans. Not gym fonts, not hospital fonts.

## Interaction And Motion Direction

Motion should feel restrained and premium: subtle fades, smooth section reveals, no flashy gimmicks. The site should feel fast, calm, and deliberate.
