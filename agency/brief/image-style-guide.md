# BioDesign Image Style Guide

Use this file before sourcing or generating images.

## Visual Direction

- Mood: premium clinical, confident, warm, aspirational without fitness-culture energy.
- Lighting: warm clinical lighting, natural light where possible, soft shadows.
- Color grade: slightly desaturated, warm highlights, deep dark shadows.
- Camera angle: eye level.
- Subject matter: confident clothed adults ages 35-55, consultation settings, modern clinic interiors.
- Backgrounds: clean medical consultation rooms, warm modern offices, refined clinical interiors, subtle abstract biological textures.
- Wardrobe: business casual, tasteful casual, professional but not sterile.
- Avoid: shirtless, gym equipment, supplements, bodybuilding, blue LED lab lighting, cold stock medical imagery.

## Site-Wide Consistency Rules

- Keep lighting consistent across major page images.
- Keep color grade aligned with the brand palette.
- Avoid visual concepts that conflict with compliance rules.
- Prefer images that support the conversion story, not generic decoration.
